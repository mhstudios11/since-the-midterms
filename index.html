Since the Midterms: Day 14

Democrats won both chambers of Congress on November 3. Here is what has happened since then, at home and around the world: late race calls, a new fight in Washington, the Iran war, and the news that hasn't stopped. Newest first, with sources on every item.

    Contacting Your Rep

    When contacting your rep, say you're a constituent and give your ZIP code, name the bill, and how you want them to vote.

    Example: “Hi, this is {name here} and I’m calling from {zip code} about H.R. 1234. I want you to vote {yes/no}.”
